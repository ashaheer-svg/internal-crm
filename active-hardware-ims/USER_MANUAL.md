# ActiveIMS - User Manual

Welcome to the **ActiveIMS** User Manual. ActiveIMS is a comprehensive Inventory and Business Management System designed for Active Solutions, specialized in hardware inventory, CRM pipelines, and service contract management.

---

## 1. Dashboard Overview
The Dashboard is your command center, providing real-time metrics and urgent alerts.

![Dashboard Overview](file:///C:/Users/shahe/.gemini/antigravity/brain/f717dc49-d73f-497f-b323-2a38cfee5a8e/dashboard_main_1772787366951.png)

### Key Features:
- **Urgent Attention**: High-priority alerts requiring immediate action (e.g., low stock, pending RMAs).
- **Brief Summary**: A snapshot of your internal inbox for workflow notifications.
- **Business Metrics**: Total Products, Available Stock, Total Stock Value, and Pending RMAs.

## 2. CRM & Project Management
The CRM module tracks your sales pipeline from initial lead to final shipment.

![CRM Pipeline](file:///C:/Users/shahe/.gemini/antigravity/brain/f717dc49-d73f-497f-b323-2a38cfee5a8e/crm_projects_pipeline_1772787384659.png)

## 5. Enterprise Transactions
The hub for all commercial documents and fulfillment orchestration.

![Delivery Orders](file:///C:/Users/shahe/.gemini/antigravity/brain/f717dc49-d73f-497f-b323-2a38cfee5a8e/delivery_orders_list_1772787406281.png)

### Transaction Types:
- **Delivery Orders (DO)**: Orchestrate stock reservation, packing slips, and dispatch.
- **Purchase Orders (PO)**: Manage procurement from suppliers.
- **Invoices**: Record revenue and link to Delivery Orders for seamless financial tracking.
- **Transaction Log**: A full audit trail of all inventory movements (Receipts, Issues, Transfers).

---

## 6. Technical Build Workflow
When a Delivery Order is confirmed, it enters the **Technical Build Queue**.

### Workflow Stages:
1. **Ready for Build**: Technical team receives a notification to assemble and test items.
2. **Built**: Once completed, Sales and Account Managers are notified that the order is ready for shipment.
3. **Item Rejection**: If a serial number is found defective during build, it is quarantined to RMA, and the Sales team is alerted to provide a replacement.

---

## 7. Messaging & Notifications
ActiveIMS features an automated internal notification system.

- **Role-Based Alerts**: Notifications are sent to specific user categories (e.g., `TECHNICAL`, `SALES`, `ACC-MGR`).
- **Detailed Content**: Each message includes the End Customer, Partner, ordered items, and expected delivery dates.
- **Acknowledgment**: Users can track receipts to ensure all stakeholders have seen the update.
